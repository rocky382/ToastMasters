Use these expert Toastmasters techniques to elevate your delivery during practice:

1. Master the Strategic Pause

Instead of using filler words like "um," "ah," or "you know," stop completely for 1-2 seconds. A deliberate pause makes you sound authoritative and controlled.

2. Vocal Variety & Energy

Change your tone and speed across segments. Speak energetically during The Hook, slow down clearly for The Point, and use descriptive tone during The Story.

3. Anchor Eye Contact

Pick 3 virtual spots (left, center, right) in the room. Complete a single full thought or sentence while looking at one spot before shifting your focus.

4. Land The Drop Strong

Never end with "that's all I have." End on a clear key message or call to action, pause for emphasis, and conclude cleanly with "Thank you, Toastmaster."
